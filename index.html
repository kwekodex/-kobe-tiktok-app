Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Applies to: Kobe Aussie TikTok Uploader Effective: 25 August 2026

This page covers a small personal-use tool that publishes videos to one TikTok account, the account for Kobe, an Australian Shepherd, and his dog-trick content. It is not a public product and is not offered to other users.

Terms of Service

What this is

The Kobe Aussie TikTok Uploader is a script operated solely by its developer to publish pre-edited videos to a single TikTok account that the developer owns and controls. It connects to TikTok only through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Who can use it

Only the developer. This tool is not distributed, is not available for other TikTok accounts to authorize, and has no signup flow for third parties.

What it does

  • Uploads a video file to the connected TikTok account.
  • Sets the post's caption, cover, and privacy setting at upload time.
  • Does not read, modify, or delete any other content on the account.

No warranty

Provided as-is, for personal use. The developer may change or discontinue the tool at any time without notice.

Privacy Policy

Data this tool touches

The only account data involved is the developer's own TikTok authorization token, obtained through TikTok's standard OAuth flow and used exclusively to publish the developer's own videos. The tool does not access, collect, or store data belonging to any other TikTok user, viewer, or commenter.

No third-party data collection. This tool has no users other than its developer. It does not run analytics, does not sell or share data, and does not have a database of visitors, followers, or viewers.

Where things are stored

Authorization tokens are stored locally on the developer's own machine, not on any shared or third-party server. Video files are the developer's own edited footage.

Third parties

The only third party involved is TikTok itself, via its official API, under TikTok's own privacy policy and terms of service.

Contact

Questions about this tool can be directed to the developer via the connected TikTok account.
